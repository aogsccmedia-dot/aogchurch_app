About us

A church that feels like home.

We're an Assemblies of God family in Woodmead — ordinary people from every walk of life, gathered around the Word of God, the presence of the Holy Spirit and a table where everyone has a seat.

01 · Bible-based

Rooted in the Word

Scripture is our foundation. We teach the Bible clearly and practically, so it shapes how we live on Monday, not just on Sunday.

“Your word is a lamp to my feet.” — Psalm 119:105

02 · Community-centred

Family before formality

We do life together — sharing meals, carrying burdens and celebrating wins. Nobody walks in alone and nobody walks out unseen.

“They devoted themselves to fellowship.” — Acts 2:42

03 · Spirit-filled

Alive in worship & prayer

We're Pentecostal and expectant. We make room for the Holy Spirit to move, heal and send us into our city.

“Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit.” — Zechariah 4:6

Your first Sunday

What to expect.

When and where do you meet?

We gather every Sunday at 17 Humber Street, Woodmead, Sandton. Service times are on the Services & events page and in the weekly letter.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from traditional attire to jeans and sneakers. Come as you are.

What happens in a service?

Heartfelt worship, prayer, and clear, practical Bible teaching. Services usually last about two hours, and there's always time to connect afterwards.

Is there something for my kids and teens?

Yes. We have ministry for children and a lively youth ministry, with leaders who love them well and keep them safe.

How do I become part of the church?

Fill in the short Join the church form (or tap Google to do it in seconds). A leader will reach out personally within the week to welcome you.
