How to use our church app.

Simple steps with pictures. Take your time: you can't break anything. If you get stuck, ask anyone at the welcome desk, or message a leader from Messages inside the app.

1Open the church website2Sign in3Become a member4Your Home5Get a ticket for an event6Fill in and tap Get Ticket7Read today's verse8Ask for prayer9Vote10Settings and signing out11Put the app on your phone

Step 1

Open the church website

  1. On your phone or computer, open the internet (Chrome, Safari or Samsung Internet).
  2. Type aogsccyouth.com at the top and press Go.
  3. You'll see our church's front page. You don't need to sign in to look around.
Back to the list

Step 2

Sign in

  1. Tap Join at the top right, or the little person at the bottom.
  2. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Gmail account. That's all: no new password to remember.
  3. No Gmail? Ask anyone at the welcome desk on Sunday and they'll help you.
Back to the list

Step 3

Become a member

  1. The first time, we'll ask a few short questions. Most of it is already filled in for you.
  2. Tap the answer that fits you, then Next.
  3. When you finish, a leader will welcome you and confirm your membership.
Back to the list

Step 4

Your Home

  1. After signing in you always start on Home.
  2. At the top are your buttons: Home, Messages, Devotions and More (Vote, Store, Settings and the rest). Tap one to go there.
  3. My tickets shows your tickets. Coming up at church shows what's next. Swipe left and right to see more.
Back to the list

Step 5

Get a ticket for an event

  1. On Home, tap an event under Coming up at church.
  2. Read when and where it is, then scroll down to Get your ticket.
Back to the list

Step 6

Fill in and tap Get Ticket

  1. Check your name and phone number, and choose how many tickets.
  2. If the event costs money, pay by EFT using the reference shown, then add a photo of your proof of payment.
  3. Tap the Get Ticket button. Your ticket comes to your email and appears on Home, ready to show at the door.
Back to the list

Step 7

Read today's verse

  1. Tap Devotions at the top.
  2. Read the verse, think about the question, and pray.
  3. Tap I've read it & prayed.
Back to the list

Step 8

Ask for prayer

  1. Open Prayer (from Home, under What to know).
  2. Write your request. You choose whether only the prayer team sees it, or it goes on the prayer wall.
  3. Every Monday, members get the week's prayer list by email to pray together.
Back to the list

Step 9

Vote

  1. Tap Vote at the top. Voting is for confirmed members.
  2. When there's a vote, you'll see the roles to fill. Tap a person, then Cast my vote. Your vote is secret.
  3. We email everyone before voting starts, to pray first.
Back to the list

Step 10

Settings and signing out

  1. Tap Settings at the top to choose which emails you get.
  2. Tap Sign out (top right) when you're done, especially on a shared phone.
  3. Signing out takes you back to the church website.
Back to the list

Step 11

Put the app on your phone

  1. On the website, open the menu and tap Get the app.
  2. Follow the two or three steps for your phone. The church icon appears on your home screen, like any other app.
  3. Tap the icon any time to open it. No app store needed.
Back to the list

Still stuck?

That's okay. Speak to someone at the welcome desk on Sunday, or call or WhatsApp the church office. We're happy to sit with you and set it up together.
