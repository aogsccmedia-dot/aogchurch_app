About this website

The home of AOG Sandton City Church online

aogsccyouth.com is the official website and members' app of AOG Sandton City Church, 17 Humber Street, Woodmead, Sandton. Everything below is free to browse, with no account needed.

  • Church information: who we are, what we believe, service times and how to visit.
  • Events & tickets: get tickets for services and events, pay by EFT and receive digital tickets.
  • Joining & membership: join the church family and manage your membership and profile.
  • Prayer & the Daily Word: share prayer requests and read a daily Bible verse together.
  • The weekly letter: an encouraging Sunday email with the week's services.
  • Optional Google sign-in: only to create your account and pre-fill forms. We receive your name, email and profile picture, nothing else. See our Privacy policy and Terms of use.
