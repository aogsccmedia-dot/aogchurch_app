FreeCreator, free for every church.

Built by AOG Sandton City Church

Songs, scripture, pictures and video on your projector, with a stage screen, phone remote and livestream output. Free and open source, made by AOG Sandton City Church for Windows and Mac computers.

Try FreeCreator Online now, in your browser

Checking places…

What it does

Everything for Sunday, nothing in the way.

Songs in seconds

Paste lyrics and get clean slides. Import from FreeShow, OpenLP and text files.

Bible on screen

Type “John 3:16” and it's there. Five free translations that keep working offline.

Every screen

It finds your projector, TV and stage screen, with their sharpness and refresh rate.

Phone remote

Move slides from your phone. Stage screen with clock, timer and messages for the band.

Built for ATEM

Switch cameras, keyers and macros, start the ATEM's stream, and put the song words on your stream automatically.

Every screen its own job

Projector, side TVs, stage screen and words-on-black for keying, all from one show.

OBS made simple

Big scene buttons, Go live, Record and mutes. One tap adds your words to OBS.

Bring your songs

Finds FreeShow and imports everything in the background, even after a restart.

Sound and pads

Choose the speaker, clear-voice and warm-music settings, and worship pads in any key.

Helpful AI

Tidy pasted lyrics, find verses for a topic and turn sermon notes into slides.

Share services

Send a whole service to another computer, as a file or over your church Wi-Fi.

Good to know

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 or 11, and Mac computers (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer). It's built for laptops and desktops; there's no phone version. On older computers it switches to Light mode by itself to keep slides and video smooth.

When can I download it?

The beta opens on 1 February 2027. There are no downloads before then. Reserve one of the first 20 places now and we'll email you the day it opens.

How many people can join the beta?

The first 20. Each tester gets a personal beta key so their feedback comes straight to our team.

Can I use our church's songs?

Yes. Import or type the songs your church sings. Modern songs are copyrighted, so show them under your church's CCLI licence, as with any presentation software.

Is it really free?

Yes, and it always will be. It's open source under the MIT licence, so anyone can use, share and improve it.
