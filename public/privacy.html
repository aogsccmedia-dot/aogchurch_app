Privacy notice

How we look after your information

AOG Sandton City Church ("we") respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you join, RSVP, send a prayer request or contact us, we collect the details you choose to give — such as your name, date of birth, contact details, area, school or work, faith journey, ministry interests, emergency and guardian contacts, and any photo or documents you upload.

Why we collect it

To welcome you, follow up personally, care for you pastorally, keep you safe at events and camps, connect you to ministry teams, and let you know about gatherings using the channels you choose.

Who can see it

Only authorised church and youth leaders, through a password-protected dashboard. We never sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing. Prayer requests marked "pastors only" are restricted to pastoral leaders.

Where it's stored

Securely on Cloudflare's infrastructure, encrypted in transit. Access is logged.

Under-18s

If you are under 18, we ask for a parent or guardian's details and consent.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, or withdraw consent for communication. Use the contact form on our home page and a leader will help you. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
