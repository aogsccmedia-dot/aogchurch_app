Cookie policy

Cookies & on-device storage

We keep this simple: no advertising cookies and no tracking. Here's the little we do use, and why.

Last updated 9 October 2026

Essential cookie

scc_session keeps you signed in after you sign in with Google. It's secure, can't be read by scripts, and expires after you sign out or after a period of inactivity. Without it, signing in wouldn't work.

Google sign-in

When you use the "Sign in with Google" button, Google may set its own cookies to make sign-in work. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Functional storage (your choice)

Only if you allow Functional cookies, your browser remembers a draft of the join form while you fill it in, and whether you've dismissed the "Get the app" tip. This stays on your device and is never sent to us until you submit a form.

If you install the site as an app, your phone also keeps a copy of the pages so they open quickly and work with a weak connection.

No tracking or ads

We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics.

Your choices

You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Clearing the session cookie will sign you out.
