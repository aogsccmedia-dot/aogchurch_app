How to use it on a Sunday

  1. Sign in and open itOn the laptop connected to the projector. Chrome or Edge works best.
  2. Build your serviceAdd songs (type, paste or import FreeShow / OpenLyrics files), scripture by reference, and text or sermon slides. The AI can tidy pasted lyrics and turn sermon notes into slides.
  3. Open the outputClick Output. A window opens: drag it onto the projector screen and press F11 for full screen. A stage screen window is there too.
  4. Go liveClick a slide to show it. Use the arrow keys for next and previous, B for a black screen.
  5. Stream (optional)Click Live to open the Studio: pick your camera (or ATEM / HDMI capture), test the internet, add lower thirds, paste your stream URL and press Go live.

Online

  • Songs, scripture (5 public-domain translations), text and sermon slides
  • Themes, logo and backgrounds (images and short videos)
  • Projector and stage screen windows
  • Shows you can save and share as files
  • Open-source AI help: tidy lyrics, sermon slides, scriptures, lower thirds, stream titles and painted backgrounds
  • Studio: cameras, HDMI capture cards and an ATEM Mini over USB, six scenes, animated lower thirds, background music, recording and live streaming (WHIP, e.g. Cloudflare Stream, which can forward to YouTube and Facebook)
  • An internet check that picks the right stream quality

Desktop app only

  • Several projectors and outputs at once, OBS and green screen
  • Full ATEM switcher control (cuts, keyers, macros) and OBS
  • Phone remote over Wi-Fi
  • Bulk import from folders, works fully offline
