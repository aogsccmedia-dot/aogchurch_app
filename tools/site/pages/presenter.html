Good to know

Which computers does it run on?{{i:plus}}

Windows 10 or 11, and Mac computers (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer). It's built for laptops and desktops; there's no phone version. On older computers it switches to Light mode by itself to keep slides and video smooth.

Why does my computer show a warning the first time?{{i:plus}}

The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS show a warning once. Windows: click More info → Run anyway. Mac: Control-click the app → Open, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. After that it opens normally.

How many people can join the beta?{{i:plus}}

Only the first 20. Each tester gets a personal beta key so their feedback comes straight to our team. The beta runs until 31 December 2026, and the full release arrives on 31 January 2027.

Can I use our church's songs?{{i:plus}}

Yes. Import or type the songs your church sings. Modern songs are copyrighted, so show them under your church's CCLI licence, as with any presentation software.

Is it really free?{{i:plus}}

Yes, and it always will be. It's open source under the MIT licence, so anyone can use, share and improve it.
