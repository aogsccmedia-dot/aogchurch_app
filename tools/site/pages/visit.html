Questions people ask

When are the services?

Everyone is welcome every Sunday, plus prayer, cell groups and the youth service during the week. See services & events for times.

Where is AOG Sandton City Church?

17 Humber Street, Woodmead, Sandton, Johannesburg. There is parking on site.

What kind of church is it?

A community-centred, Bible-based, Spirit-filled Pentecostal church, part of the Assemblies of God (AOG) family in South Africa.

What should I wear? Do I need to book?

Come as you are. Weekly services are free and need no booking; some special events have tickets online.

How do I join the church?

Fill in the short join form and a leader will reach out personally.
